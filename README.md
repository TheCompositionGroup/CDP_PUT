# CDP_PUT
**Structured geopolitical-to-market prediction method**  
**The Composition Group | Concept Development Package | Level 1 — Explore**

## Decision question
Can a preregistered, structured geopolitical analysis method provide measurable, reproducible forecast skill beyond appropriate informational baselines—and, separately, provide information not already reflected in market prices?

This is **method-development research**, not a trading system or investment recommendation. No investor funds or live capital are authorized. The project structure currently specifies **$0 capital until N-Gate clears**; a separate explicit gatekeeper authorization would be required before any subsequent experiment.

## Current stage
**DEFINE → MEASURE (current) → ANALYZE**. DESIGN and VERIFY are outside Level 1 scope. The canonical source structure remains in [CDP_PUT — Overall Project Structure v1.0](./CDP_PUT%20%E2%80%94%20Overall%20Project%20Structure%20v1.0).

## Working sequence
1. Define eligible forecast questions and objective resolution sources.
2. Preregister baseline selection and freeze forecast packets before the information cutoff.
3. Estimate feasible independent event families and define a realistic primary endpoint.
4. Clear or fail N-Gate **before making confirmatory claims or authorizing capital**.
5. Conduct independent forecasts with analyst provenance, evidence packets and frozen hashes.
6. Resolve outcomes append-only; compare Brier scores, calibration and reproducibility.
7. Present the TCG decision page: PROCEED / PROCEED CONTROLLED / REFINE / HOLD / PIVOT / STOP.

## Controls
- Distinguish information **available at cutoff** from outcome information learned afterward.
- Market-implied *risk-neutral* probabilities are not interchangeable with real-world outcome probabilities.
- Treat overlapping questions or event families as potentially dependent; do not inflate effective sample size.
- Unresolved and rejected forecasts remain visible; do not silently remove misses.
- Exploratory results are hypothesis-generating until a prospective validation plan is preregistered.

## Status
Framework and templates established; **no predictive validity or market edge is claimed**. All evidence, power assumptions and decision thresholds remain to be documented and approved.
