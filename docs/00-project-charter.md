## 1. Problem
Geopolitical narratives frequently appear plausible without demonstrating predictive accuracy against simple reference classes or market expectations. The concept must demonstrate that its probability estimates are objectively resolvable, reproducible and superior to an appropriate baseline before a larger build is justified.

## 2. Gatekeeper decision
**Does the evidence justify controlled further development?** This is a development decision, **not** a decision to deploy trading capital.

## 3. In scope
- Specify eligible forecast questions with objective 90-day-or-shorter resolution.
- Separate actor, event, transmission and market forecasts.
- Prepare information-cutoff evidence packets, analyst method traceability and immutable forecast records.
- Preregister historical/reference-class baselines and document blinded economic benchmarks when relevant.
- Determine sample-size/power feasibility and limits on independent observations.
- Measure predictive skill, calibration, resolution coverage and second-analyst reproducibility as feasible.
- Report limitations, kill triggers, risks and next actions.

## 4. Out of scope
- Production software or an interactive multi-party simulation.
- Portfolio construction, solicitation or provision of personalized investment advice.
- Any live trades or deployment of capital during Level 1.
- Claims of profitable arbitrage based solely on forecast divergence.
- Retroactive reclassification of questions to improve reported performance.

## 5. Deliverables
1. Short client concept book with scope boundary on page 2.
2. Controlled CDP with method, evidence, calculations and adverse findings.
3. Asset index covering forecast packets, timestamp proofs, data, charts and computations.
4. One-page decision and gatekeeper turnover listing owners and dates.

## 6. Stage and kill gates
- **K1:** Objective resolution is impossible or too unreliable.
- **K2:** Obtainable independent sample is insufficient for the proposed test.
- **K3:** No meaningful skill over the informational reference baseline.
- **K4:** Unacceptable calibration for intended use.
- **K5:** The method cannot be independently reproduced.
- **K6:** Actor/event insight fails to transmit into a market-specific prediction.
- **K7:** Any apparent edge fails after pricing, spread, risk premia and execution uncertainty.

The **primary endpoint, minimum useful effect, thresholds and statistical decision rules must be approved before the confirmatory sample is evaluated**. A null or insufficient sample is not proof of zero effect.

## 7. Decision rules
**PROCEED:** Requirements met with adequate prospective evidence.  
**PROCEED CONTROLLED:** Evidence warrants a narrowly bounded follow-on test.  
**REFINE:** Feasible but protocol or measurement defects need correction.  
**HOLD:** Evidence, authority or sufficient sample unavailable.  
**PIVOT:** A different outcome, market or method offers a more testable question.  
**STOP:** A decisive kill criterion fails.
